ⓘ How Does Government Application Tracking Work?

Whenever you submit an application at a government e-Seva centre, Village Panchayat, or state portal, you receive an Acknowledgement Receipt containing an Application Reference Number (ARN or EID).

  • Stage 1 - Submission: Your application details and documents are entered into the government database.
  • Stage 2 - Field Verification: The Village Administrative Officer (VAO) or Revenue Inspector (RI) verifies your local residential address and income.
  • Stage 3 - Approval: The Tahsildar or Zonal Officer reviews the report and applies a digital signature.
  • Stage 4 - Download: You can download the certificate online or collect a printed copy from the e-Seva centre.
